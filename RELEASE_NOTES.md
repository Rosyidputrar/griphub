## Bahasa Indonesia

- Chat panjang diringkas otomatis: begitu konteks melewati batas, giliran lama menjadi ringkasan dan giliran terbaru tetap utuh. Sebelumnya model berkonteks besar baru meringkas saat hampir mencapai batasnya sendiri. Batas bawaannya kini mengikuti model: 65% dari jendela konteksnya (minimal 300 ribu token), bukan lagi angka yang sama untuk semua model. Batasnya bisa diubah di Pengaturan → Usage → Ringkas chat pada; pilihannya juga mengikuti jendela model yang dipakai, sampai di bawah batas model.
- Saat konteks diringkas, chat menampilkan "Meringkas konteks" dengan waktu yang berjalan, lalu berapa token yang dihemat (misalnya 320k → 24k token). Bila gagal, alasannya ditampilkan. Ketuk barisnya untuk penjelasan.
- Pesan yang dikirim saat konteks sedang diringkas kini menunggu sampai selesai, tidak lagi ditolak.
- Animasi memuat yang baru di seluruh aplikasi: komet yang berputar, besar saat satu layar menunggu data dan kecil di tombol atau baris. Warnanya menyesuaikan tema terang dan gelap, dan berhenti bergerak bila animasi dimatikan di sistem. Pembaca layar mengucapkan "Memuat…", lalu "Selesai" atau "Gagal".
- Memulihkan cadangan kini diakhiri tanda centang saat berhasil. Bila memulihkan cadangan, menyiapkan deploy, atau memulai chat gagal, dialognya menampilkan tanda silang sesaat sebelum alasannya muncul.
- Pesan error kini ditulis dengan bahasa yang mudah dipahami: koneksi putus atau terlalu lama memberi tahu apa yang perlu dilakukan, dan teks teknis tidak lagi tampil. Error yang memenuhi layar (Usage, Box, Template) punya tombol Coba lagi.
- Lebih mudah disentuh: tombol kecil di kolom chat, header beranda, pilihan segmen, dan chip di Buat gambar kini punya area sentuh 48 dp tanpa berubah tampilan.
- Pembaca layar kini menyebut nama setiap sakelar (misalnya "Kunci aplikasi, sakelar, aktif"), bukan hanya "sakelar".
- Teks merah, hijau, dan oranye di mode terang lebih pekat sehingga mudah dibaca.
- Bilah navigasi beranda tidak lagi terpotong di layar HP yang sempit (320 dp).
- Teks yang belum diterjemahkan di Cari, Git, dan Sinkron GitHub kini berbahasa Indonesia.
- Kartu persetujuan untuk mengedit atau menulis file kini menampilkan perubahannya (baris yang dihapus dan ditambah) sebelum kamu mengizinkan. Perubahan yang panjang bisa dibuka utuh di halaman sendiri. Berlaku juga untuk persetujuan subagent.

- Tombol Tolak/Selalu/Izinkan tidak lagi terpotong di layar sempit dengan teks besar.
- Review & commit: setelah agent selesai dan ada file yang berubah, chat menawarkan "Review & commit". Satu layar menampilkan semua file yang berubah beserta diff-nya, pesan commit yang ditulis agent (bisa diubah), serta tombol Commit atau Commit dan push. Membatalkan perubahan agent juga bisa dari layar yang sama. Tersedia juga di menu Project.
- Mode server: pesan yang dikirim saat koneksi ke server putus kini disimpan dan dikirim otomatis begitu tersambung lagi, berurutan, alih-alih gagal. Pesan ini tetap tersimpan walau aplikasi ditutup. Bila server menolaknya (misalnya budget habis), alasannya ditampilkan dengan tombol Kirim lagi, dan pesan sesudahnya, termasuk yang baru diketik, menunggu di belakangnya. Pesan yang koneksinya putus saat sedang dikirim tidak dikirim ulang otomatis, karena server mungkin sudah menerimanya. Kamu diminta mengecek dulu, supaya tugas tidak jalan dua kali. Membatalkannya mengembalikan teksnya ke kolom chat.

- Giliran lama yang sudah diringkas bisa dibaca lagi: ketuk baris "Konteks diringkas" lalu "Baca giliran sebelumnya". Pesan, jawaban agent, dan tool-nya tampil apa adanya dan bisa disalin sebagai Markdown.

## English

- Long chats are summarized automatically: once the context passes the limit, older turns become a summary and the newest stay as they were. Large models used to summarize only close to their own limit. The default limit now follows the model: 65% of its context window (at least 300k tokens), no longer one figure for every model. Change it in Settings → Usage → Summarize the chat at; the choices follow the open model's window too, up to just under its limit.
- While the context is summarized, the chat shows "Summarizing the context" with a running clock, then how many tokens it saved (for example 320k → 24k tokens). A failure says why. Tap the line for what it means.
- A message sent while the context is being summarized now waits for it instead of being refused.
- A new loading animation across the app: circling comets, large when a whole screen is waiting and small on buttons and rows. The colours follow the light and dark themes, and it stands still when animations are turned off in the system. Screen readers say "Loading…", then "Done" or "Failed".
- Restoring a backup now ends with a check when it worked. When restoring a backup, preparing a deploy or starting a chat fails, the dialog shows a cross for a moment before saying why.
- Error messages are in plain words: a lost or slow connection says what to do, and technical text no longer shows. An error that fills a screen (Usage, Box, Templates) has a Try again button.
- Easier to tap: the small buttons in the chat box, the home header, segmented choices and the chips in Generate image now have a 48 dp touch area, without looking any different.
- Screen readers say what each switch is for (for example "App lock, switch, on"), not just "switch".
- Red, green and orange text in light mode is deeper and easier to read.
- The home navigation bar no longer overflows on narrow (320 dp) phones.
- Text left in English in Search, Git and GitHub sync is now translated.
- The approval card for editing or writing a file now shows the change (lines removed and added) before you allow it. A long change opens whole on its own page. Subagent approvals show it too.
- Type @ in the chat box to pick a project file: matching files show as you type, and the path goes into your message.
- Share a chat: from the Project menu, share it as text or a Markdown file, copy it, or save it to Downloads. It holds the messages, the agent's answers and the tools it used.
- Deny/Always/Allow no longer break apart on a narrow screen with large text.
- Review & commit: when the agent finishes and files changed, the chat offers "Review & commit". One screen shows every changed file with its diff, a commit message the agent writes (yours to edit), and Commit or Commit and push. Undoing the agent's changes is on the same screen. Also in the Project menu.
- Server mode: a message sent while the connection to the server is down is now kept and sent by itself, in order, once it is back, instead of failing. It is kept even if the app is closed. If the server refuses it (no budget left, for example), it says why with Send again, and the messages after it, new ones included, wait behind it. A message whose connection dropped while it was being sent is not sent again by itself: the server may already have it, so you are asked to check first and the task doesn't run twice. Cancelling it puts its text back in the chat box.
- Files can be sorted by name, recently edited, largest or type, with folders kept on top or not. Chips under the title show one kind of file: folders, code, images, documents, data, archives or media. Only the kinds the folder has appear.
- The "Context summarized" line now also says what the summary cost (for example ~0.42 credits): a summary is a model request too.
- Turns that were summarized can be read again: tap the "Context summarized" line, then "Read the earlier turns". The messages, the agent's answers and its tools show as they were, and can be copied as Markdown.
- A summary that fails is no longer tried again after every turn until the daily budget is gone: it is tried again only once the chat has grown by a quarter, and not after three failures in a row.
- Server mode: when the agent on the server restarts while you are in a chat, that chat opens again instead of switching to an empty one that says "Connect a model". Subagents that were working then show as lost, not as running for good.
- A subagent waiting for an approval now stops waiting when its time limit is up. Deleting a chat also stops its subagents.
- Tasks: a new "Background jobs" section shows every chat's background jobs (running ones first), how long they have run, and Stop. Tap one to open its chat.
- At most 6 background jobs run at once across all chats, so the server is not overwhelmed.
- Accounts → GitHub is honest about signing in with a code: when the app has no built-in Client ID and you have not entered one, it says that way is off and to sign in with a token. A saved Client ID now actually shows.
- Server guide: a new entry for commands that hang while the box is on (the box is stuck, not the agent), with how to stop the box or turn it off.
- The done animation (the green check) now changes colour first, then moves, as in the approved design.
- Previews of pages and dev servers on this phone can be opened. Android refuses plain http by default, so every preview of 127.0.0.1 or localhost failed before it connected. Now only those two addresses are allowed; connections to anywhere else must still be encrypted.
- Server mode: when the box is stuck (the container shows "Running" but no command answers), a command is now refused within about 30 seconds with a message that says what to do (Reset the box, or turn it off), instead of hanging with no word. The command is never run outside the box. The check runs once before a command, then is remembered for 30 seconds.
- Signing in to GitHub with a code is now waited on by the agent, not by the app. Before, if the app was closed, the screen was left, or Cancel was pressed before you approved on github.com, the waiting stopped for good and no token was ever saved. Now the agent keeps waiting until the code expires (15 minutes): approving later still completes the sign-in, and the account shows by itself. Opening the Accounts screen again shows the code again. Only the Cancel button stops the wait. A link that drops during the wait is no longer taken for a failure.
- The Accounts screen says whose accounts these are. The phone's agent and the server's agent each keep their own, so an account connected while the phone's agent was in use is not on the server, and the other way round.
- Security: the agent can no longer write outside the project folder without approval in ways that used to slip through: a leading `@` on a path, a `file://` address, or a link (symlink) inside the project that leads out. Writing into a `.git` folder now asks too.
- Security: the git the app runs on project folders (the Git panel, the project list, commits, deleting a project) no longer runs programs named in that project's git settings (for example `core.fsmonitor`, hooks, `core.sshCommand`, filter or diff drivers). When a project's settings name a program that cannot be turned off, git is not run there and the reason is shown: open the `.git/config` file and remove the entry. Tokens no longer go along to git processes either.
- Server mode: a command in the box can no longer overwrite the server's files (such as `authorized_keys` or the store key) through links planted in the box's home folder. The git and ssh settings are now sent through `podman exec` instead of being copied from the server, and sent again only when they change or after 30 seconds.
- Server setup: when the server already has a new enough Node.js somewhere else (for example `/usr/bin/node` from apt or NodeSource), the script now makes the `/usr/local/bin/node` link the service looks for. The service used to fail to start (status 203) on such servers. A link that is already there is never replaced: if it is not a new enough Node, the script stops and says why.
- Git: projects that use Git LFS now upload their large files on push too. The app runs git with hooks off, so LFS's `pre-push` hook never ran and the remote only got pointers with no files behind them. LFS settings that run programs (extensions, a custom transfer agent) are refused like other git settings that would, while what `git lfs install` writes (`--skip-smudge` included) is accepted.
- The agent no longer freezes for tens of seconds reading a very large message (for example several photos at once): the line splitter is now linear.
- A web page built to freeze its reader (hundreds of KB of `<`) no longer makes the agent stop answering while it is turned into text; the time drops from minutes to milliseconds, with the same text out. An HTML character reference that is no character (such as `&#99999999;`) no longer makes the reader fail.
- Server mode: an agent stuck in one loop and no longer answering is now killed after two minutes (`GRIPHUB_WATCHDOG_MS`) and the service restarts it; before, it hung until someone logged in over SSH.
- Sign-ins and settings are safer: the storage files (GitHub/Vercel/Cloudflare sign-ins, SSH hosts, settings, MCP servers, schedules, sync) are written whole or not at all, with the last good copy kept beside them as `.bak`. A file that cannot be read (cut short, or saved with another key) is no longer taken for an empty one and written over: it is moved to `.corrupt-<time>`, the last copy is used when there is one, and the chat shows a notice. Automatic backups leave out a store that cannot be read (it used to be saved as `null`, which then overwrote the real data when restored).
- Switching chats or rebuilding the session now builds the new session first; if that fails, the open chat keeps working instead of becoming a dead session. Two switches at once run one after the other.
- An approval answered while the link to the server is down is no longer lost: the card goes back into the queue and says why. While the link is down the Deny/Always/Allow buttons are off and the card says the agent is still waiting for your answer.
- Stop, Plan mode and the thinking level now tell you when they fail. Stop with the server link down explains that it cannot reach the server, and a Stop that went through says "Stopping…".
- A mistyped GripHub key now reads like a wrong key ("The key was not accepted. Check that you copied all of it, with no spaces.") instead of the router's raw HTTP reply in English. A router that does not answer, and other failures, are explained too.
- Bypass all permissions: the dialog now says the setting applies to all chats until you turn it off in Settings > Approvals, its button is danger-coloured, and while it is on the chat shows a "Permissions bypassed" bar with a Turn off button. The Approvals setting also says it is prompts for common risky actions, not a security boundary.
- The Tasks screen now says what a background run may do without asking: on the phone it runs with no approval whatever the setting is, on a server risky actions are refused.
- Review & commit: every file has a checkbox, and the commit contains only the ticked files (it used to run `git add -A` and take everything, including a `.env` never looked at). A file that looks like a secret (`.env`, private keys, credentials) starts unticked and is flagged. The push button names its target: "Commit and push to origin/<branch>".
- The daily budget and tool-call limits now show as errors in your language with your number format, with a "Raise limit" button that opens Usage. Carrying out a plan, starting a subagent and generating a picture now respect the daily budget too (before, only sending a message and retrying checked it).
- Security: web_fetch no longer reaches this machine, private networks or a cloud server's metadata service, including through redirects. Reading files that hold secrets (the store key, tokens, ssh keys, `/proc/<pid>/environ`) is now asked about first, and refused in an unattended run. These are lists, not a wall: a clever shell command can still get past them.
- The suggestion cards on an empty chat are no longer cut off on a 360 dp phone or with large text.
- Bottom menus (the chat and project menus, file menus, Share chat, attachments) now scroll to their last row, also with the phone on its side; "Delete" used to be cut off and out of reach.
- The welcome screens and the API key screen no longer overflow on a small phone, a phone on its side or with large text. Skip is easier to hit, and the page dots tell a screen reader which page this is.
- Text that was still English in the Indonesian UI (Files messages, "Committed", the empty chat list, server counts, "preparing…", CI step and database row counts) is now translated.
- With a server set up, the chat's title is no longer squeezed on a narrow phone or with large text: Terminal, Deploy and Project tools move into a More menu, and the connection badge becomes an icon when space is very tight.
- The accent colour choices work with a screen reader (each says its colour and which is chosen) and have 48 dp touch targets; their title no longer breaks into syllables.
- The copy button under a reply now has a label, a tooltip, a 48 dp touch target, and announces "Copied".
- Danger buttons (delete, stop) read in the dark theme, and the green, orange, pink and teal accents now read as text in the light theme.
- A chat list that fails to load now shows the error with Try again, instead of staying blank or spinning forever.
- A long streaming reply no longer gets heavier as it grows: finished parts are not re-parsed every frame, and it updates about 30 times a second.
- Very long diffs (thousands of lines) open at once: only the rows on screen are built.
- The status bar icons follow the theme on every screen, Home and the welcome screens included.
- Small controls (Home's category chips, the search toggles, the terminal's new tab, the connection badge, the Chats selection bar) have 48 dp touch targets.
- Animations across the app follow the system's remove-animations setting.
- The Chats tab stays light with hundreds of chats: only the rows on screen are built, and they are not rebuilt every time the agent reports.
- Screen readers now hear what the agent is doing, how a tool went (done, failed, running), whether a panel is open, the names of attachments and photos, and a switch row's title only once.
- On tablets, foldables and phones on their side the content keeps a readable width, message bubbles stop widening, and project tiles add columns.
- Back on the Chats, Skills or Projects tab now goes to Home instead of closing the app.
- Pill text, icons on coloured tiles, code comments, the Connect card in the dark theme and the CI spinner now have enough contrast.
- Text size is held to 2.2x (the app's setting times the system's used to reach 2.5x).
- Illustrations always show once loaded, instead of sometimes missing.
